How We Work

Craft in four careful steps

Every cup is the product of hundreds of small decisions made long before it reaches your hands. Here's how we think about the process from seed to sip.

Source
We visit farms personally, paying above Fair Trade prices to build relationships that last decades.
Roast
Small-batch roasting in our open roastery, dialled to highlight each origin's unique character.
Dial In
Our baristas taste every batch. Every grinder setting, every dose, every shot is logged and refined.
Serve
Your cup arrives at the ideal temperature, prepared with the attention it — and you — deserve.
